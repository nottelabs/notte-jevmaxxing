# Notte browser and model demos

The app lets viewers watch Jev and a Cerebras-hosted model act on concrete tasks. Existing surfaces include the browser inspector and Wikipedia race. The no-turns chess surface is a direct model-control experiment, not a browser-use demo.

The audience is developers and people watching a short product video. Priorities are a clear shared outcome, visible decision timing, truthful model labels, easy recording, and a compact dark interface. Chess specifically uses instant moves, independent model loops, per-piece cooldowns, and restrained arcade feedback. Neither a chess engine nor a scripted policy chooses moves for the providers. This is an illustrative live matchup, not a general benchmark.
